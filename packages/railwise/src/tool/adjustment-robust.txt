Run deterministic IGGIII-style robust least-squares adjustment.

Use this after preliminary adjustment and gross-error screening when residuals indicate suspected outliers but the workflow needs a stable solution without deleting observations blindly.

Inputs:
- unknowns: ordered unknown parameter names.
- equations: indirect-adjustment equations with coefficients, observed value, and optional base weight.
- k0/k1: IGGIII standardized-residual thresholds. Observations below k0 keep full weight; observations above k1 retain only minWeightFactor.
- maxIterations/tolerance: convergence controls.

Output:
- final unknown values and precision statistics.
- residuals with base weight, final weight, weight factor, and standardized residual.
- downweighted observations.
- iteration history, convergence flag, and sigma0 statistics.

Do not invent observations or weights. If the source is COSA .in2, CSV, NASEW, or loose rows, call tool_format_converter first and pass its next.args here.
